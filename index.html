⌂ You are looking at whole cities and towns. The teen birth rate is published only at this level to protect people’s privacy: where there are only a few births, the numbers could point to real people. Think of the idea behind privacy rules like HIPAA, even though these are public counts, not anyone’s records. Only compare the teen birth rate with other city or town data.

ⓘ Why are there two tabs?

Census tracts stand in (as a proxy) for neighborhoods within a city. The “Census tracts” tab maps them, so you can compare neighborhoods inside Chelsea with neighborhoods in other places.

Cities and towns: to protect people’s privacy, the teen birth rate is published only for whole cities and towns, so it is in the “Cities and towns” tab, with the housing and CDC numbers for each whole city or town.
